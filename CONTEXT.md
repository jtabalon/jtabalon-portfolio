# Personal Site

This context defines the public portfolio's professional communication and interaction language. It keeps content hierarchy and motion decisions aligned around a calm, credible experience for hiring managers.

## Language

**Primary contact action**:
The visually dominant invitation to email Joseph about a senior production-ML role. It appears in both Hero and Contact with the label “Email me.”
_Avoid_: CTA, primary button

**Secondary link**:
A navigation, evidence, professional-profile, or utility link that remains visually subordinate to the Primary contact action.
_Avoid_: Secondary action, alternate CTA

**Editorial motion**:
Restrained, purposeful motion that provides interaction feedback or softens the homepage’s initial arrival without delaying or moving reading content. It excludes scroll reveals, parallax, pulsing, and choreographed text entrances.
_Avoid_: Animation system, decorative motion
