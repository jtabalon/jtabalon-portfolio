# Website direction

## Audience and outcome

The site helps hiring managers confidently shortlist Joseph for senior production-ML roles. It demonstrates technical judgment through concise, Public-safe evidence and makes email about a relevant role the Primary contact action.

The current public identity is **Senior Data Scientist**, centered on building machine-learning systems that hold up beyond the notebook: rigorous modeling and evaluation through reliable, secure deployment.

## Editorial and content direction

- Maintain the warm editorial foundation: off-white canvas, near-black text, spacious composition, hairlines, restrained surfaces, and limited monospace.
- Keep email as the only prominent filled action. Navigation, evidence, and professional-profile links remain Secondary links.
- Keep the focused homepage structure: Hero, Published Work, Career Snapshot, About, and Contact.
- Keep Work, About, and Contact directly accessible on mobile without introducing a menu.
- Present the portrait in the Hero and omit availability, clearance, shorthand metadata, and public-resume download from the main message.
- Use first person in Hero, About, and Contact; use concise neutral formatting for chronology and publication details.
- Use Editorial motion only for purposeful feedback and a subtle one-time arrival. Exclude scroll reveals, parallax, pulsing, and delayed reading content.
- Keep public content versioned with the application until a secure publishing system is genuinely needed.
- Keep Blog, Admin, and sign-in outside the public route surface until they are deliberately redesigned and secured.

## Evidence boundaries

- Start with already-public research and original portfolio work. Removing names from restricted material does not make it safe to publish.
- For security-conscious employment, publish employer, title, dates, and a restrained production-ML summary unless each additional claim is explicitly cleared.
- Exclude clients, systems, data feeds, infrastructure, operational metrics, clearance context, and sensitive implementation details by default.
- Separate publicly provable attribution from Joseph's confirmation when writing a Contribution statement. Do not imply sole ownership or responsibility for model design or training without specific evidence.
- Do not feature the prior Raft hiring exercise in its existing form. Any future project inspired by its reliability lessons must use an independently framed problem, data source, product, and repository.
